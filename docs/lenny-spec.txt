The Lenny Mode spec:

A real person will pick up the phone on station 24 or call in from an
outside line. We assume the operator will connect the caller to one of
our game stations or dial one of our pre-defined outside numbers. If
the operator dials some other outside number, the game should still answer,
but in this case use the "real" Lenny voice.

The actor residing at the called station or outside establishment
should answer in an appropriate way. For example, Mary Smith (in the
Imperial Suite) could say, "Hello? Who is this?" Lundquist could say,
"Ahoy! Lundquist here!" (a nod to Bell himself, who wanted people to
answer with "Ahoy!" The guy at V16 could say "V16 automobile company
of America."

Then the actor responds to speech by the caller by iterating through
the standard Lenny utterances. (I don't know the Lenny game well. Does
he choose utterances at random? And can you obtain a list of the
utterances?) The actor continues in this mode until the calling party
disconnects, at which time the called station or trunk hangs up.

Thornill is a special case. He should answer with this:
Hi! Mellow greetings, ukie-dukie!

His first "Lenny" utterance, after the caller speaks, should be this:
Hey, you remind me of a man.

Then he should rotate through the following utterances in this order:
The man with the power.
The power of hoodoo.
You do.
Remind me of a man.
