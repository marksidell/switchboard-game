500 Set Impedance: Bench Checklist

551 Switchboard Simulator. Purpose: the 500 set's off-hook impedance, 100 Hz–20 kHz, to size an analog low-pass in station 24's loop that keeps the pilot tones (6–9 kHz) out of the earpiece. Date   Set (model / date stamp)

0. Before you start

  • Close WaveForms on the Windows machine (only one program can hold the AD3).
  • AD3 + Impedance Analyzer adapter plugged into portland USB, if Claude is to run the sweeps.
  • Bench supply is floating (isolated outputs). The AD3's ground is portland's ground.

1. Loop current on the switchboard (reference)

  • 500 set on jack 18 (station 24, room 203). DMM in series with one leg, mA range.
  • Lift the handset. Loop current: mA

2. Bench feed

  • 24 V supply through 200 Ω in each leg (2 W parts: up to 0.7 W each with the line shorted).
  • Measured: supply V   R tip leg Ω   R ring leg Ω
  • Phone on L1/L2, handset lifted. Bench loop current: mA  (should be near step 1)

    • Optional, more accurate: 48 V through 2 × 600 Ω, if on hand. The feed is in parallel with the phone, and a larger feed resistance makes subtracting it more accurate. Note which was used.

3. Connect the AD3

  • Impedance adapter across the phone's L1/L2 through a DC blocking capacitor: ≥ 10 µF, film or bipolar, ≥ 50 V. Value used: µF
  • Compensation with the capacitor in circuit: open (leads apart) and short (across the phone's terminals).
  • Settings: reference 1 kΩ, amplitude 100 mV, 100 Hz – 20 kHz, average 5.

4. Two sweeps

  • Feed alone: phone unplugged, feed and capacitor still in place. Save as feed.txt
  • Feed + phone off hook: handset lifted, loop current flowing. Save as 500set_offhook.txt
  • Loop current during the second sweep: mA

Save to kicad/electret/measurements/ (on Windows C:\s\sbgame\kicad\electret\measurements), the same WaveForms text format as source_500set.txt. Add a #Note: line saying what was connected. Claude removes the feed from the second sweep to get the phone alone.

5. Hand over to Claude

  • Tell Claude: wired / AD3 on portland (Claude runs the sweeps), or the two files saved.
  • Give the readings above: the two loop currents, the feed's V and R, and the capacitor.
