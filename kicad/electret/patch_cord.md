# Patch cord — two-prong headset plug

Wiring for a patch cord with a two-prong headset plug. Recorded by Mark, 2026-10-19.

| Conductor | Contact |
|---|---|
| red | tip a |
| green | tip b |
| black | sleeve a |
| white | sleeve b |

The tips carry the red/green pair and the sleeves the black/white pair, as in the
headset cord table in `README.md`.
