Fault
    The line cannot hear its own pilot, or hears more than one, for a second; a file missing; a trunk fault

  
Ringing
    Ring on, flashing

  
Calling
    A station off-hook, the operator not on it and patched to nothing: nobody has answered

  
Connected
    Patched to another circuit, named under the label

  
Operator
    The operator on the line

  
Busy
    A station off-hook or a trunk seized, and nothing above

  
Idle
    None of these

  
Where more than one applies, the higher wins. A dimmed board
    means the controller or the audio program is not reporting.
