Trunk 8: Feed from the Grandstream ATA

Trunk board rev 2, trunk 8 only (sheet 9, parts 9nn). Spec: trunk_circuit.md §4.5, Rev 5.53 · 2026-10-22. Trunks 1–7 are unchanged.

Before you start

  1. No game running. Take trunk 8 out of service in host/etc/sbgame/game.toml. It stays out until the game knows trunk 8 is the outside line.
  2. Power off. Check the current values: R906 220 Ω 2 W 1%, R907 150 Ω.
  3. Parts: one 330 Ω, ≥0.5 W, 5% is fine (for R906), plus hookup wire: two short jumpers and two leads to the ATA.

Changes

  1. Pull K901 (relay). Then jumper pole 1 COM to pole 1 NC (TIP to node A) and pole 2 COM to pole 2 NC (RING to node B) . Leave the NO holes empty: they go to the ring bus. Pin numbers are on the J104D2C5VDC datasheet or the silkscreen.
  2. Remove D903 (1N4007). This takes the board's current source off the line.
  3. R906: replace the 220 Ω with 330 Ω, with the GND leg lifted. The node B leg stays in its pad. The other leg goes to the ATA's ring, not to GND.
  4. ATA's tip into the K901 pole 1 NC hole (node A).
  5. U901 to its AC input:
    • Lift pin 2 (DC+) and pin 3 (DC−) clear of their pads. Both must be open.
    • Pin 1 to R907's free end (the end that went to pin 2).
    • Pin 4 to R906's lifted leg (the ATA ring).
    • Pins 5, 6 and 8 stay as they are.
    Don't leave pin 2 soldered with pin 1 jumpered to it. That shorts one diode of the bridge, and one polarity then misses the detector.

Don't: ring trunk 8 with the switchboard's ring key. That puts about 90 Vrms into the ATA's FXS port, and nobody knows whether it survives (hence the spare). With K901 pulled the board cannot ring trunk 8. The game must never ring, tone or play files on trunk 8.
